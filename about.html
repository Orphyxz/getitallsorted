Hi, I’m Lindsey!

I am passionate about helping people declutter and sort out their homes. After over 15 years of helping friends and family members get organized, I finally decided to start my own business as a professional home organizer back in California. I have worked with a wide variety of people in a wide variety of situations, from full-on hoarding to smaller, more focused projects such as closets or kitchens.

I have discovered that I really love helping people get their living spaces under control, no matter how small or how extensive the project may be! Now I’ve moved overseas to Europe, and am continuing to build my business and make new friends. It’s an honor to be invited into a client’s home, and it’s always a good time helping someone get organized. I’ve got many satisfied clients on both sides of the Atlantic who can attest to that!

If you know you need help, but are afraid of being overwhelmed by traditional “Marie Kondo” style professionals, I can assure you that my priorities during any project are always respect and empathy. I never throw anything away without explicit consent, and I don’t steamroll or guilt-trip anyone into getting rid of cherished mementos.

My services are always tailored to your needs, and completely flexible depending on the kind of help you want, from simple motivational presence to hands-on sorting and tidying.
